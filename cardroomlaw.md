## Current Project: CELLTOWER

CELLTOWER is the current expression of this long-term thread. It is a single-file, NES-accurate Tetris trainer for iPhone Safari that also functions as a cryptographic verification system. Every game produces a compact, replayable, hash-chained stream (v3.0) designed to prove that a specific sequence of human decisions occurred under measurable time pressure.

The deeper objective is infrastructure for **Proof of Human Cognition** — the ability to mathematically demonstrate that a performance reflects genuine human decision-making rather than automated or reconstructed behavior. This has applications in skill-based competition, training data for AI systems that need to model human cognition under constraint, and long-term economic models that reward verifiable human skill.
