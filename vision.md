# Vision for CELLTOWER

CELLTOWER is not just another Tetris game.

It is the latest chapter in a 30+ year journey with Tetris that began in 1991 when I created **Yet Another Tetris Clone (YATC)** on the Amiga. After serving as President of NanoTech Gaming and developing the BetCube platform, I am now bringing those lessons into the world of pure skill-based competition under California law.

### Core Philosophy

We believe skilled humans should be able to earn money from their skill.

CELLTOWER is designed as a **neutral platform**, not a traditional gambling house. The operator only takes a small, transparent service fee to cover costs. Average players should roughly break even. Truly skilled players can win real money from less skilled players.

This creates a self-funded system where skill is genuinely rewarded.

### Legal Approach

CELLTOWER is built conservatively to honor both the letter and the **spirit** of California gaming law:

- Pure skill-based gameplay (no chance elements)
- Small fixed entry fees
- Conservative payout limits (currently capped at 2x to stay clearly legal)
- Clear separation between the game operator, money handling, and players

We intentionally keep things simple and clean so the system can grow responsibly and remain trustworthy.

### What We Are Building

The current version is the **crawl phase**: a high-quality, mobile-first NES-style Tetris game optimized for iPhone and Safari.

Every game generates a cryptographically verifiable replay stream designed to prove:
- A real human played the game
- The score is legitimate
- The performance reflects genuine human decision-making and timing

This is the foundation of our "Proof of Human Cognition" system.

### Target Audience

CELLTOWER is built for multiple groups:

- Hardcore Tetris players looking for the best mobile experience
- Competitive players who want to put real stakes on their skill
- Street-level competitors who currently play informal cash games (the "digital street craps" use case — two people, one phone, cash on the table, clear winner takes the pot)
- Anyone who wants a legal, fair way to turn skill into money

The experience should feel direct and real — like two guys putting cash down and letting the phone decide who played better.

### Long-Term Vision

We are building more than a game. We are building infrastructure that can verify human skill and cognition in a provable way.

Starting with Tetris, the same technology and economic model can eventually expand to many other skill domains.

This project is about creating new, legal opportunities for skilled humans to be rewarded in an AI-heavy future — while staying ethical, transparent, and fully aligned with California law.
